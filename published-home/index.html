Your Mac.
Your folder.
Your ChatGPT plan.

Morrow runs on your Mac and works inside the folder you pick. The thinking happens on your own ChatGPT plan.

  1. Runs on your Mac.Conversations, transcripts and the files Morrow makes stay on your Mac. Your ChatGPT sign-in stays on your device.
  2. You choose what it can touch.Pick one work folder. Morrow changes files only inside it unless you switch on Full access. macOS asks you before Morrow can use the microphone, the screen or other apps.
  3. AI runs on your own ChatGPT plan.Your request and the context it needs go to OpenAI through Codex, under your account's data controls. Aestrum does not automatically receive your conversations or files. A support report you choose to send can include private work in its screenshot or description.
