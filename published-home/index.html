Before you
start.

Is there a free trial?+

When a trial is available, its length and first charge date appear before you authorize payment. Download Morrow to review the current offer.

How does UPI AutoPay work?+

You authorize recurring payments through Razorpay. Checkout shows the amount and scheduled charge date before you approve. Cards are also supported.

Can I cancel?+

Yes. Manage or cancel in Morrow's Account & plan settings or your online account. Cancel an available trial before its first charge to avoid that charge. Paid access lasts until the end of the paid term.

Do I need ChatGPT?+

Yes. Connect your own ChatGPT account during setup. Morrow uses Codex under that account, so your plan's access and usage limits apply. Your ChatGPT subscription is separate from Morrow. Morrow never asks for an OpenAI API key.

Is Morrow listening all the time?+

No. It listens after you press Speak, or press control and option to open the Island. Press again to stop. You can type instead at any point.

Can I use one account on two computers?+

Yes, from Morrow 0.3.27. Stay signed in on up to five computers. One of them uses Morrow at a time, and you can move it to whichever computer you're at.

Which Macs are supported?+

The Mac download is for Apple silicon, including M1 and newer, on macOS 13 or later. The build is notarized by Apple. Follow the install guide for setup and permission help.

Which PCs are supported?+

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Download the setup, run it, then sign in. The Windows setup page covers company-managed laptops too.

Why does Windows warn me about the installer?+

The Windows setup isn't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, check that the file is Morrow-Setup-0.3.27.exe, then Run anyway. If there is no Run anyway option, Smart App Control is on: never switch it off for Morrow, contact support instead.
