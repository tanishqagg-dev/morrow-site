# Open launch evidence

- Confirm in writing that Morrow may distribute and bill for the intended account-based OpenAI/Codex inference path. ChatGPT identity sign-in alone is insufficient.
- Test a clean, signed, notarized Mac install, Keychain access, first voice exchange, account connection, entitlement, and restore. The installed Pilot reached a Keychain activation error during this review.
- Publish and test one real Razorpay consumer plan and its renewal, cancellation, refund, failed-payment, and webhook paths before showing a buy button.
- Observe at least 10 novice Mac users doing the first voice task without coaching. Measure comprehension of two bills, permission requests, and time to first useful result.
- Test an actual speech provider or local speech stack for latency, cost, language coverage, noisy rooms, and accessibility before choosing it.
- Clear the Morrow name and relevant marks in intended launch markets.
